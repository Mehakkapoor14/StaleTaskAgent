# Customer Portal Relaunch — goals (excerpt from pm/charter.md)

**Objectives (FY26 H2)**
1. Self-service billing GA by 30 Nov 2026: customers view and download invoices, see tax breakdowns, and update payment methods without contacting support. Target: billing support tickets down 40%.
2. Enterprise SSO (Okta, Azure AD) for all enterprise accounts by 31 Dec 2026.
3. Close all critical and high pen-test findings before the SOC 2 audit on 15 Jan 2027.
4. Meet legal obligations (GDPR data export) without further delay.

**Out of scope**
- Native mobile apps (re-evaluate in FY27).
- Any work on the legacy reporting dashboard — retired 30 Jun 2026; reporting now lives in the analytics product.
- Internet Explorer 11 support (unsupported browser since 2022).
